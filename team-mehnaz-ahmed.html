Meet Mehnaz

Mehnaz is a dedicated Special Education Consultant and Parent Resource Specialist who brings a unique blend of strategic leadership, specialized educational expertise, and firsthand parenting experience to our team. She currently serves as the Department Head of Special Education, a Middle School Co-Teacher, and a Reading Interventionist at a charter school in Fulton County, Georgia, where she has been teaching since 2020. Her extensive academic background includes a Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT) in Special Education from Kennesaw State University, a specialized Dyslexia Endorsement, and a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the University of Massachusetts. Driven by the philosophy of Ignacio Estrada — “If a child can't learn the way we teach, maybe we should teach the way they learn” — she works closely with families to bridge the gap between school systems and home. Mehnaz tailors educational strategies to match each child's unique learning profile, helps parents navigate complex education and funding systems, and guides them toward vital special education resources.

Mehnaz firmly believes that growth is a mindset and that we are all lifelong learners, a principle that guides her empathetic approach to supporting parents. Outside of her professional roles, she is the proud mother of three wonderful children. When she is not advocating for students or collaborating with families, she enjoys recharging at the beach, exploring local restaurants, and creating lasting memories with her family.
