Physical Therapy

Our licensed physical therapists help children build strength, balance, coordination, and gross motor skills for everyday movement and play.

We treat developmental delays, torticollis, gait abnormalities, and low muscle tone using play-based exercises that keep kids engaged while they build the skills to run, jump, climb, and explore with confidence.

Ages served: Birth to 18 years.

Session length: 45 minutes.
