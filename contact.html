Common Questions

Q. Do you accept insurance?

A. Yes! We accept most major insurance plans including Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, Humana. Contact us to verify your benefits.

Q. How long does a typical session last?

A. Most sessions are 45–60 minutes. The frequency and duration are determined during your initial evaluation based on your child's goals.

Q. Do I need a referral to start therapy?

A. No referral is needed to contact us or schedule a consultation. However, some insurance plans may require one for coverage — we'll help you navigate that.

Q. Do you offer teletherapy?

A. Yes, we offer secure, HIPAA-compliant teletherapy sessions for families who prefer remote care or cannot travel to our clinic.
