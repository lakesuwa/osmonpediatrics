Occupational Therapy

Our occupational therapy services help children develop the skills needed to participate more independently and confidently in everyday life. From sensory processing and emotional regulation to fine motor skills, self-care, play, and social participation, our therapists work closely with children and families to address challenges while celebrating progress. Using a child-centered approach, we create engaging experiences that help children build confidence, improve daily functioning, and reach meaningful milestones in both home and community environments.

Ages served: Birth to 18 years.

Session length: 1 hour.
