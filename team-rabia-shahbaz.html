Meet Dr. Shahbaz

As an educator for more than 20 years, I have dedicated my career to helping children and families reach their full potential. My journey has taken me through both secondary and higher education, where I have had the privilege of teaching, mentoring, and supporting students from diverse backgrounds and learning abilities. For the past nine years, I have also successfully operated an after-school tutoring and exam preparation center, helping students build strong academic foundations, develop confidence in their abilities, and discovering that they are capable of achieving more than they ever imagined.

Throughout my career, I have learned that every child learns differently. Some require extra time, some need a different approach, and many simply need someone who believes in them. My passion has always been to meet children where they are, recognize their strengths, and provide the tools, encouragement, and support they need to thrive.

While my professional experiences shaped my understanding of education and child development, my greatest inspiration came from my personal journey as the mother of an autistic child.

Becoming a parent of a child on the autism spectrum opened my eyes to the challenges and triumphs that so many families experience every day. It taught me the importance of advocacy, patience, acceptance, and community. It also revealed how difficult it can be for families to navigate multiple services, appointments, and resources while trying to meet the needs of their child and maintain the well-being of the entire family.

That experience inspired me to create Osmon Pediatric Therapy & Wellness, a center built on the belief that every child deserves to feel valued, supported, and included, and that every family deserves a place where they can find both services and community.

At Osmon Pediatric Therapy & Wellness, our goal is to provide comprehensive support under one roof. We want families to have access to high-quality therapies, wellness resources, and enrichment opportunities without feeling overwhelmed by the process. More importantly, we want every child and family who walks through our doors to feel welcomed, respected, and embraced as members of our community.

My hope is that Osmon Pediatric Therapy & Wellness becomes more than a therapy center. I hope it becomes a place where families find support, children discover their strengths, parents feel understood, and everyone is reminded that they belong.

Thank you for trusting us to be part of your family's journey. We look forward to growing, learning, and celebrating every milestone alongside you.
