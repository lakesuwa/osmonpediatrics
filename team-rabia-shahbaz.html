Meet Dr. Shahbaz

I have spent more than 20 years as an educator, working with children of every age and every kind of learner. Over that time, one truth has stayed with me above all others: every child learns differently, and some children need a different approach entirely.

For nine years, I ran an after-school tutoring and exam-preparation center, where I worked closely with students and their families to build confidence, close learning gaps, and prepare for some of the biggest tests of their academic lives. It was during those years that I began to see, again and again, how much a child could grow when the support around them was tailored to who they actually were — not a one-size-fits-all lesson plan.

That belief became personal when I became the mother of a child on the autism spectrum. Suddenly I was no longer just an educator looking in from the outside; I was a parent navigating therapy schedules, school meetings, and the search for providers who truly understood my son. I quickly learned how exhausting it can be for a family to piece together speech therapy here, occupational therapy there, and wellness support somewhere else entirely — all while trying to hold everyday life together.

That experience is what led me to found Osmon Pediatric Therapy & Wellness. I wanted to build the place I wish had existed for my own family: somewhere children could receive therapy, enrichment, and family support all under one roof, in a space designed around how they actually learn and grow.

Today, Osmon brings together speech, occupational, and physical therapy, feeding support, sensory play, enrichment classes, and family wellness coaching — because we believe children thrive when their care is connected, not scattered, and when their families are supported every step of the way.

To every family who has trusted us with your child's care: thank you. It is an honor to walk this journey with you, and it is why we do this work every single day.
